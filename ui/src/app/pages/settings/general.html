Controls

Two-finger swipe What a trackpad two-finger swipe does in the 3D view.
Palm rejection Ignore the hand resting on the screen while drawing with an Apple Pencil or stylus, so the palm never orbits or zooms the 3D view. Finger-only gestures are unaffected.
Scene diagnostics chips Show FPS and WASM timing chips in the viewer overlay.

3D View

Field of view Perspective camera angle. Lower feels flatter and more zoomed-in; higher shows more of the scene with stronger perspective.
Anti-aliasing Smooths jagged edges. Auto disables it on high-density displays where it barely helps. Changing this reloads the 3D view.
Render resolution Higher renders sharper on high-density displays but costs more GPU; lower favours frame rate.
Use filament color for models Color model meshes with the active filament profile color instead of the neutral viewer tone.
